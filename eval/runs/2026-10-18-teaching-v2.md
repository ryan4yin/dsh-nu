# Teaching v2: bash-habit rewrites

Date: 2026-10-18

This round turns the refusals found in two hours of real sessions into eval
cases, reproduces them on the old guides, and checks the rewrite on the new
ones. It follows the layered workflow in `eval/README.md`: the old version
reproduces first, then a small model on the failing cases, then the larger
model, then acceptance over the remaining cases.

## What changed

| text | before | after |
|---|---:|---:|
| `compact.md` | 1,044 chars | 1,881 chars |
| `full.md` | 4,059 chars | 5,248 chars |

Both guides gained a pre-send scan that names the exact refused spellings:
`2>`, `&>`, `&&`, `mkdir -p`, `ls -R`, a bare `$name =`, and
`sh -c` / `bash -c`. The compact guide also says not to add `2>/dev/null`
even when a task says to ignore errors.

## Cases reproduced from the sessions

Seven cases, each targeting a refusal class seen in the logs:
`proc-inspect` (system probing, errors to ignore), `tool-presence`
(`||` / `command -v`), `deep-mkdir` (`mkdir -p`), `merge-streams`
(`> f 2>&1`), `search-ignore` (`grep ... 2>/dev/null`), `recursive-list`
(`ls -R` / `ls -lR`), and `chain-step` (`&&`).

## Layer 2: fix on the key cases

Small model (ds41f), four cases that reproduced (`deep-mkdir`,
`search-ignore`, `recursive-list`, `chain-step`), compact arm, 5 samples:
0 refusals in 20 samples.

Larger model (gpt-6-luna), same four cases, both arms, 3 samples: 0 refusals in
24 samples.

## Layer 3: acceptance

- Remaining cases (`proc-inspect`, `tool-presence`, `merge-streams`), both
  arms, 3 samples: ds41f 0/18; luna 1/18. The one refusal is
  `uname -r; (grep MemTotal /proc/meminfo || true); ...` in the compact arm,
  refused as `and-or`; the retry was clean.
- Stable-case smoke (small model, `json-active`, `stderr-discard`,
  `background-wait`, both arms, 2 samples): 0 refusals in 12 samples.

Post-fix total: 1 refusal in 92 samples. Tokens: the key-case layer used
1.06M (ds41f compact) and 1.08M (luna both arms); acceptance used 0.81M and
0.85M; the regression smoke used 0.58M.

## Reading

- The old guides reproduce the session problem: 22 refusals, spread over
  `mkdir -p`, `ls -lR`, `2>/dev/null`, `2> /dev/null`, `sh -c`, and the
  false-positive assignment form.
- The rewritten guides reduce it to one slip in 92 samples; the remaining
  `||` is a model habit the pre-send scan still misses, and the model recovers
  on the next call.
- Stable cases were not rerun for the whole matrix; the smoke check found no
  regression, so this round did not pay for the full cross-product.
- This still does not justify changing the default guide from `full`.

Raw samples stay under `.work/eval-out`; this file is the committed record.
